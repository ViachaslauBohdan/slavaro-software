# SMS — script (EN)

Every message has **three parts**, in this order:

1. **Company** — who is writing (Volska Automation + your name).
2. **Services** — what you offer, in one or two short sentences.
3. **Callback** — a clear ask: reply to SMS, call, or YES.

**Placeholders:** `[Name]`, `[Your name]`, `[Phone]`, `[Company]`.  
**Same offer as the main email:** landing from **€200** (~48h), AI automation from **€500**; contact **volskagroup@gmail.com** | **t.me/volskagroup**

---
