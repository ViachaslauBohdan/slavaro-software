# What we do — short guide (for clients)

## In one sentence

**We build fast landing pages that bring you leads, and we set up AI so repetitive office work runs without you copying data by hand.**

---

## 1. Landing pages (48 hours)

**What it is:** A single focused web page for your offer — not a full corporate site. People land there from ads, LinkedIn, or your link in bio.

**What you get:** Looks good on phone and computer, loads quickly, shows up better in Google basics, and a contact form that **pings you on Telegram and email** when someone writes in.

**Who it’s for:** You need to start collecting real inquiries without waiting weeks for a big website project.

---

## 2. AI reporting automation

**What it is:** Your numbers and raw data get turned into **ready-to-send reports** in Google Docs or Sheets — weekly or on a schedule you choose — without you rebuilding the same tables every time.

**Who it’s for:** You spend hours every week on “the same report, different numbers.”

---

## 3. AI workflow / operations automation

**What it is:** One connected flow for **how work moves**: e.g. new leads get captured, sorted, and sent to the right place (CRM, Telegram, email); notes from calls can become drafts of proposals or follow-ups; tools like Google Workspace, Slack, or Notion talk to each other **reliably**, not by copy-paste.

**Who it’s for:** Small teams drowning in admin between tools, or sales/ops steps that always happen the same way but still eat time.
